Cosmetic and optional omissions do not block release.

If no newer safe build can be produced, deliver the most recent stable build, explain the attempted work, obstacle and proposed different approach, and await owner instructions on the blocked task. An unchanged build is not new progress. Do not silently continue repeated attempts when meaningful progress has stalled.
